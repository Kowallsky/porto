## 1. Purpose and Design Intent

Homepage Reference V3.0 defines the new visual direction for the Kowallskyy homepage.

The previous V2.0 concept — a centered portrait surrounded by two rotating technology orbits — is no longer the approved direction. V3.0 adopts the composition of the supplied ORYVEN reference: a bold editorial hero with a central portrait, small informational text blocks positioned around the portrait, a dramatic gradient rising from the bottom, and oversized typography anchored to the bottom edge.

The intended result is a premium, cinematic, blue editorial portfolio that feels confident, distinctive, and visually composed.

**Core direction:** ORYVEN-inspired editorial composition × blue cinematic gradient × personal data/AI engineering identity.

The reference is for composition and visual language. Do not copy ORYVEN's brand name, wording, imagery, or business claims.

## 4. Color Palette V3.0

The palette below is the approved direction for the homepage. Use the blue tones as the dominant identity and Rose Coral only as a restrained accent.

### 4.1 Primary palette

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Ocean Navy | `#021F2D` | Main dark canvas and upper hero background |
| Oxford Blue | `#002147` | Deep blue shadow, gradient depth, dark surfaces |
| Royal Blue | `#4169E1` | Main blue gradient transition and visual emphasis |
| Soft Azure | `#B8E1F5` | Light contrast, gradient highlight, secondary text on dark surfaces |
| Rose Coral | `#FF6C65` | Small accent for CTA, selected details, and interactive highlights |

### 4.2 Color hierarchy

Recommended visual balance:
- 55–65% Ocean Navy and Oxford Blue.
- 20–30% Royal Blue and intermediate blue transitions.
- 10–15% Soft Azure and neutral light text.
- Up to 5% Rose Coral.

These are visual guidance ratios, not rigid pixel quotas. Ensure text contrast and avoid using Rose Coral as a competing large background.

### 4.3 Hero gradient

The hero background must transition from deep blue at the top to a brighter blue glow at the bottom.

Suggested vertical gradient:
1. Top: Ocean Navy `#021F2D`.
2. Upper-middle: Oxford Blue `#002147`.
3. Lower-middle: Royal Blue `#4169E1`.
4. Bottom glow: Soft Azure `#B8E1F5`, blended into Royal Blue rather than appearing as a flat stripe.

Use a soft radial/atmospheric bloom near the lower center or lower third, behind the portrait and headline. The gradient should feel cinematic and dimensional, not like a simple four-color linear band.

Rose Coral may appear as a tiny detail in a CTA or active state, but it must not replace the blue atmosphere.

## 5. Homepage Navigation

Use a minimal top navigation aligned to the reference's restrained editorial style.

### Desktop
- Left: Kowallskyy wordmark.
- Center: `Projects`, `About`, `Experience`, `Contact` (or the approved section links already present in the site structure).
- Right: `Let's Work Together` CTA.

Navigation should be visually quiet and compact. It may use a subtle transparent surface or fine divider, but must not become a large dashboard-style bar.

### Mobile
- Keep the wordmark visible.
- Replace the full center menu with an accessible menu control.
- Keep the primary contact action available without crowding the viewport.
- Ensure no horizontal overflow.

## 6. Hero Composition — Required Layout

The hero should reproduce the structural composition of the supplied reference, adapted to Kowallskyy's identity.

### 6.1 Overall composition

- A full-width, visually dominant hero section.
- Deep Ocean Navy background with a dramatic blue gradient rising from the bottom.
- A large portrait of the owner positioned centrally as the primary visual focal point.
- Small informational text blocks placed around the portrait.
- An oversized headline positioned along the bottom edge.
- A minimal top navigation.
- A restrained CTA treatment.

The hero should feel like one carefully art-directed composition, not a collection of unrelated cards.

### 6.2 Portrait placement and treatment

- Use the owner's approved, clean standalone portrait.
- Place the portrait near the horizontal center of the hero.
- The portrait should be large enough to dominate the composition and visually connect the upper dark region with the lower gradient.
- Apply tasteful blue color grading, tonal adjustment, and lighting integration so the portrait belongs naturally in the Ocean Navy / Oxford Blue / Royal Blue environment.
- Preserve the owner's actual facial identity and recognizable appearance.
- Do not replace the owner with a stock model, generated person, fictional character, or the person shown in the ORYVEN reference.
- Do not fabricate clothing, credentials, or physical attributes.
- Do not bake text, UI, gradients, or decorative elements into the portrait asset. Keep these as separate web layers.
- If a clean portrait is not available, use a neutral, clearly marked temporary image placeholder in local preview. Do not use the ORYVEN person as a substitute.

### 6.3 Informational text blocks around the portrait

Use small, editorial-style text blocks positioned around the central portrait. They should be carefully aligned and remain readable.

**Upper-left block — Professional identity**
- `INDEPENDENT DATA & AI PRACTICE`
- `DATA ANALYTICS / AUTOMATION`
- Do not invent a city or studio establishment year.

**Upper-right block — Professional summary**
- `BUILDING ANALYTICAL SOLUTIONS AND INTELLIGENT WORKFLOWS`
- Supporting sentence may use the approved supporting statement or a concise excerpt from it.

**Lower-left block — Working philosophy**
- Use a concise, truthful statement about turning raw data into useful insights and practical workflows.
- Do not claim unverified business outcomes or client impact.

**Lower-right block — Calls to action**
- `EXPLORE PROJECTS`
- `LET'S WORK TOGETHER`
- Use the existing approved CTA destinations and labels where available.

These blocks should visually frame the portrait rather than overlap the face or obscure the subject.

### 6.4 Oversized bottom headline

The headline is a defining part of the composition.

Approved text:
`TURNING DATA INTO INTELLIGENCE.`

Requirements:
- Large uppercase display typography.
- Positioned along the bottom of the hero.
- Spans most of the viewport width on desktop.
- May visually sit over the lower gradient and behind the lower portion of the portrait only if legibility and layering remain intentional.
- Use tight but readable letter spacing.
- Scale fluidly with viewport width using responsive typography.
- Do not allow accidental clipping, unreadable overlap, or horizontal page overflow.
- If the complete phrase cannot fit on one line, use a deliberate responsive line break rather than clipping letters.

The hero may use the full headline as the oversized text, while the positioning label remains in a smaller editorial text block.

## 7. Typography

Retain the existing approved typefaces:
- **Manrope:** main headline, navigation, body copy, buttons.
- **IBM Plex Mono:** small editorial annotations, metadata, labels, and technical descriptors.

Typography hierarchy:
1. Oversized uppercase hero headline.
2. Small positioning label and surrounding annotations.
3. Compact navigation and CTA labels.
4. Supporting copy with readable line length.

Avoid making all text monospace. The small labels should contrast with the large Manrope headline.

## 8. Motion and Interaction

Motion should reinforce the editorial composition without distracting from the portrait or headline.

Recommended:
- Subtle entrance reveal for the portrait and side annotations.
- Slight opacity/translate reveal for the oversized headline.
- Gentle ambient gradient movement only if it remains extremely subtle.
- CTA hover/focus feedback using color, underline, or a small directional movement.
- Smooth anchor navigation to homepage sections.

Do not use:
- Rotating technology orbits.
- Circular text paths around the portrait.
- Orbiting technology icons.
- Continuous high-frequency movement.
- Heavy parallax that interferes with reading or performance.

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by disabling or simplifying entrance and ambient animations.

### Selected Work visual direction

- Avoid four identical boxed cards.
- Use project-specific evidence or approved project visuals.
- Use hover states for image scale, overlay, title movement, or subtle color shift.
- Keep project titles, categories, and navigation affordances clear.
- Do not invent project imagery or expose private client data.

Project detail pages continue to follow `Project Details Design Direction V1.0`; this document only defines the homepage preview treatment.

## 10. Responsive Behavior

Validate at desktop (1440px), tablet (768px), and mobile (375px).

### Desktop
- Keep the central portrait prominent.
- Keep four annotation areas around the portrait with deliberate alignment.
- Display the oversized headline across the lower hero.
- Maintain generous negative space and clear hierarchy.

### Tablet
- Reduce portrait scale and reposition annotations to avoid collisions.
- Allow headline to wrap intentionally.
- Preserve the visual relationship between portrait and lower gradient.

### Mobile
- Preserve the core visual idea: portrait, blue gradient from below, editorial annotations, and large headline.
- Do not force the desktop four-corner layout into a narrow screen.
- Reorder annotation blocks into a clear vertical sequence or compact overlay positions.
- Keep CTA controls easy to tap.
- Scale the headline fluidly and wrap deliberately.
- Prevent text from covering the face.
- Prevent horizontal overflow.

## 11. Asset Requirements

Required:

- The supplied ORYVEN reference image in this package for composition reference only.

Portrait guidance:

- If the portrait has a background, use careful background removal or blending only if the result remains natural and does not distort the subject.
- Do not use generative replacement of the face.
- If no approved portrait is supplied, retain a labeled placeholder until the owner provides one.
