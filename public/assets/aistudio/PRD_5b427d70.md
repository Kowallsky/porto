# Project Requirements Document — US Immigration Law Firms Data Collection

**Project type:** Freelance data collection / web scraping  
**Status:** Updated draft  
**Version:** 1.1

## 1. Objective

Collect and prepare a structured dataset of 500 US immigration law firms according to the client's requested final record count. Data collection used Sulekha and Google Maps as source directories.

## 2. Scope

### In scope
- Collect business listings from Sulekha.
- Collect supplementary business listings from Google Maps using MapsScraper.
- Integrate and compare information from both sources.
- Manually review or complete information that remains unconfirmed.
- Prepare a final structured dataset of 500 records.
- Provide a sample dataset for portfolio demonstration without disclosing the complete client dataset.

### Not established / out of scope
- Ongoing automated monitoring or scheduled refreshes.
- A production database or deployed data pipeline.
- A specific accuracy percentage, duplicate rate, or manual-completion count.
- Treating the two source collection volumes as 938 unique final firms.

## 3. Source collection

| Source | Method | Collection volume |
|---|---|---:|
| Sulekha | Semi-automated Python scraping | Approximately 500 |
| Google Maps | MapsScraper | 438 |

Sulekha presented CAPTCHA challenges when navigating to new locations. The collection therefore used a semi-automated approach with human interaction where necessary.

## 4. Integration and finalization

The collected Google Maps information was used as supplementary data during integration with Sulekha records. Information that remained unconfirmed was reviewed and completed manually where possible. The final client output was prepared to meet the requested 500-record target.

## 5. Sample dataset schema

The supplied sample workbook has two worksheets, each containing the same 10 sample records and 15 columns: Firm Name, Street Address, City, State, ZIP Code, Phone, Website, Practice Areas, Subcategories, Google Rating, Review Count, Source, Last Verified, Firm URL, and Notes.

## 6. Deliverables and confidentiality

- Client deliverable: final dataset containing 500 records.
- Portfolio evidence: supplied sample workbook only.
- The complete client dataset must not be published or requested for portfolio presentation.

## 7. Portfolio presentation requirements

Demonstrate multi-source collection, semi-automated scraping, integration, manual review, and structured spreadsheet output. Clearly distinguish the sample workbook from the full client deliverable.
