## 1. Portfolio Identity

**Portfolio name:** Kowallskyy  
**Professional positioning:** Data & AI Automation Engineer  
**Core message:** Turning Data Into Intelligence.

**Supporting statement:**

> Building analytical solutions and intelligent workflows through data, AI, and automation.

The portfolio presents the owner as an early-career technology professional with a foundation in data analytics and an emerging specialization in AI integration and workflow automation.

The positioning is intentionally hybrid:
- **Data Analytics** is the foundation and demonstrates the ability to collect, prepare, analyze, and communicate data.
- **AI Automation** is the differentiator and communicates the direction of continued technical development.

Do not reposition the portfolio as exclusively an AI engineer, machine learning engineer, or software engineer unless the owner explicitly approves a future change.

## 2. Portfolio Objectives

The website has two primary objectives:

1. **Professional credibility:** Give recruiters a clear view of relevant skills, practical experience, projects, and education.
2. **Freelance discovery:** Help potential clients understand the types of data and automation work the owner can undertake.

The website should demonstrate evidence rather than rely on broad claims. Project descriptions must distinguish bootcamp exercises, academic research, freelance work, and personal prototypes.

## 3. Target Audience

- Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating entry-level or junior data-related candidates.
- Business owners and prospective freelance clients looking for data collection, analysis, reporting, or automation support.
- Technical peers who want to inspect the owner's project approach and implementation evidence.

Content should remain understandable to non-technical visitors while retaining enough technical detail to establish credibility.

## 4. Core Capability Areas

Present capabilities in these three service-oriented groups:

1. **Data Collection & Web Scraping**
   - Collecting structured information from public web sources.
   - Cleaning and organizing collected data.
   - Preparing datasets for downstream analysis.

2. **Data Analysis & Reporting**
   - Data preparation and exploration.
   - SQL-based querying and aggregation.
   - Analytical visualization and dashboard reporting.

3. **AI Integration & Workflow Automation**
   - Integrating AI services into practical workflows.
   - Designing automated processes and tool orchestration.
   - Connecting services and business processes where appropriate.

These are capability categories, not a guarantee that every listed service has been delivered in a production environment. Individual claims must be supported by project evidence.

## 5. Featured Project Strategy

The homepage should feature four projects that demonstrate different dimensions of the portfolio:

1. **Automotive Sales Analysis** — Data analytics using Excel, SQL, Python, and Power BI.
2. **US Law Firms Data Collection** — Freelance web scraping and structured data collection.
3. **AI-Powered Data Analysis Workflow** — AI-assisted data analysis workflow developed in a bootcamp context.
4. **Semantic Retrieval for Running Shoes** — Academic research comparing TF-IDF and sentence embeddings for semantic retrieval.

Use the following context labels accurately:
- Automotive Sales Analysis: bootcamp project.
- US Law Firms Data Collection: freelance project.
- AI-Powered Data Analysis Workflow: bootcamp project.
- Semantic Retrieval for Running Shoes: academic thesis/research project.

Do not imply that bootcamp or academic projects were external client deployments.

## 6. Experience and Education

The portfolio may include:
- Freelance Data Collection / Web Scraping experience.
- Office Administration Instructor experience, including Microsoft Office instruction with emphasis on Excel.
- Informatics education at Universitas AMIKOM Yogyakarta.
- Relevant training and certifications.

Keep experience descriptions factual and concise. Do not invent employment dates, client names, certification credentials, or quantified outcomes.

## 7. Tone and Content Principles

- Professional, direct, concise, and evidence-led.
- Use clear language before technical jargon.
- Explain the problem, approach, tools, and outcome of each project.
- Distinguish what was implemented from what was learned or proposed.
- Avoid inflated claims such as “expert,” “industry-leading,” or “production-grade” unless independently justified.
- Do not fabricate project metrics, screenshots, clients, or business impact.

## 8. Global Website Calls to Action

Primary homepage actions:
- `Explore Projects`
- `Let’s Work Together`

Use these labels consistently unless the owner approves a copy revision.
